---
title: "Privacy"
description: "Lattice Five collects nothing. Everything stays on your device."
---

Lattice Five collects **nothing**.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains no
  third-party code and makes no network requests of its own.
- **Your games stay on your device.** Scores, streaks, and replays are
  stored locally.
- **Challenge codes contain no personal data** — a code describes a board
  layout, nothing else. Sharing one shares nothing about you.
- This website serves static pages only; it sets no cookies and runs no
  analytics.

If any of this ever changes, this page will say so plainly, and the change
will be opt-in.
