Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Here's how to reach it.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Lattice Five is developed in the open. The best place for bugs and requests
is the issue tracker:

- [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vlumi/lattice/issues)

Please include your device and OS version, and the steps to reproduce — it
makes fixes much faster.

## Common questions

**Where are my scores and replays?**
On your device, and nowhere else. Every finished game is kept as a full
replay you can revisit from the History tab.

**Can I play on Mac, iPhone, and iPad?**
Yes — it's one free app across all three, from a single App Store listing.

**How do challenge codes work?**
Every generated board has a short code (shown in the header). A friend can
enter it under **Enter Code…** — same code, same board, anywhere. There is
no server; the code itself describes the board.

**What are 5T, 5D, 4T, 4D?**
Rule variants: lines of **5** or **4** dots, with collinear lines allowed to
**T**ouch at endpoints or required to stay **D**isjoint. 5T is the classic.
The 4-dot games are fully solved — perfect play reaches exactly 62 (4T) and
35 (4D), if you can find it.
